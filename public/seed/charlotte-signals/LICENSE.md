# Traffic signal seed snapshot: City of Charlotte

`signals.json` in this folder is a snapshot of the operating traffic signals in the City of
Charlotte "Traffic Signals" layer:
https://services.arcgis.com/9Nl857LBlQVyzq54/arcgis/rest/services/Traffic_Signals/FeatureServer/0
https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=3bf079185338440fbb7febe81dd5d9a9
