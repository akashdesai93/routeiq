# RouteIQ · UI directions

Three different looks for the RouteIQ iPhone app, each shown as the same three
screens (Pick a route, Driving, The automatic switch) plus a looping animation
of the moment the app finds a faster route and switches by itself.

Built with the [Stageshow](https://benji.org/stageshow) skill: plain React and
CSS, its phone frame, stage and motion parts, and its filming scripts for
checking the result. This folder is separate from the app; nothing here is
wired to the real code.

## In the app repo

This workspace lived in a local-only repo (`routeiq-design`, last commit
`e2cb543`) and was copied into the app repo as `design/` so cloud sessions can
continue it. It is still its own project: run every command below from
`design/`. The root app's `npm ci`, typecheck, lint, tests and e2e ignore it.

Not carried over:

- `shots/`: the rehearsal frames (hundreds of PNGs, ~150 MB). They are scratch
  output, regenerated by `scripts/film.mjs` and `tools/stills.sh`,
  `tools/loops.sh`, `tools/merged.sh`. Git-ignored.
- `reference/mapbox-screenshots-internal-not-for-publishing/`: Mapbox /
  OpenStreetMap stills for #47. This repo is public, so they are never
  committed (git-ignored here and in the root `.gitignore`). Recapture them
  locally from the web prototype if you need them; `tools/patch47-draft.py`
  expects them at that path.
- Third-party brand and app artwork from the Stageshow kit is not vendored here (public repo); fetch the upstream skill if a scene needs it. This covers `kit/brands/`, the skill's `assets/app-icons.ts`, `playground/` and every `examples/` study except `choreography`; see the skill's `NOTICE.md`.
- `exports/comparison-loop.gif` (7.5 MB, over the 5 MB limit) and the
  per-direction folders `exports/clearway/`, `exports/noodle/`,
  `exports/vector/` (superseded by the merged direction). `npm run export`

## The merged direction (the one the owner chose)

Decided in GitHub issues #21 and #22 on the app repo. Code in `src/merged/`,
exports in `exports/merged/` (`npm run export:merged`), stages at
`?capture=merged-compare`, `merged-plan`, `merged-pick`, `merged-drive`,
`merged-more`, `merged-switch-pending`, `merged-switch`, `merged-drive-day`,
`merged-switch-day`, `merged-signals`, `merged-loop`.

Decisions recorded:

- **Base is Vector**: black glass, Barlow Condensed numerals, amber as the one
  "news" colour. Amber appears only for a pending or confirmed switch. The
  primary button is white, so amber never means anything else. No mono
  microcopy on anything shown while driving; sentences are Inter.
- **Everything sits on the map**: a turn card at the top, a sheet at the
  bottom, the street to turn onto visible and named. The native app will use
  the Mapbox Navigation SDK; the map here is a hand-drawn generic street grid
  in this project's own styling (no tiles, no network, no other product's map
  style).
- **Pick a route**: time leads, lights second, distance last. The delta row
  stays (−8 min · −2 lights · −3 mi).
- **Driving**: the hero is the next turn and its distance; one line holds the
  ETA and the arrival time. Two numbers read at a glance (0.8 mi, 35 min); the
  arrival time is deliberately smaller and dimmer. Lights to go, distance,
  auto-switch and voice are behind **More**.
- **Copy** is Clearway's: "Switches by itself if a faster route holds up."
- **Signal states**: full ("21 lights"), partial ("≥ 9 lights", "Some signal
  data is missing"), unknown ("Lights: unknown"). Never 0 when unsure; the
  delta row says "Lights: can't compare" instead of a number.
- **The switch** (#22): the current route goes grey with its slow stretch
  hatched and labelled, the new branch is amber where it peels off. Pending is
  a hollow amber marker and an outlined banner; confirmed is a filled marker
  and a filled banner. The banner is the spoken sentence, word for word
  ("Switching: saves 8 minutes, turn in 0.8 miles."). The numbers add up:
  43 → 35 min, arrive 10:24 → 10:16. **Keep my route** is offered in both
  stages. Noodle's "no taps" line is gone.
- **Daylight**: the same layout flips to black on white with a pale map. Amber
  is only ever a fill under black text there, and the amber branch gets a
  black casing, because amber on a light map is about 1.6:1.
- **Plan** (new): "Where to?", "From: my location", Home and Work as empty Add
  chips with no addresses, four recents.

Where the two issues pulled against each other or against readability:

- #21 allows two glanceable numbers; #22 needs before and after ETA, before
  and after arrival, the saving and the turn-off distance on the switch
  screen. Resolved by hierarchy: 0.8 mi and the new 35 min (amber chip) are
  the two large figures; the old values are small, dim and struck through;
  the saving lives in the spoken sentence.
- The spoken sentence names 0.8 miles, so the turn card holds 0.8 for as long
  as the banner shows and only counts down after it closes.
- "Zero taps" against "Keep my route": the switch still happens by itself;
  the button only declines it.
- Before the switch the turn card reads "Straight on" with no distance,
  because the brief gives one turn only. The native app would show the real
  next manoeuvre.

### After "Keep my route" (#43)

- Pressing **Keep my route** in either stage puts the driver back on the route
  they were on: the amber branch and marker go, the ETA returns (43 min,
  10:24), and a calm, non-amber banner confirms it.
- The banner's first line is the spoken line, word for word: **"Staying on
  your route."** The second line is on screen only: "Won't offer that route
  again for 10 min." (the engine's `noReturnSec` = 600).
- The exception is not put on the driving screen: inside the 10 minutes the
  route is offered again only if the saving has doubled. It then arrives as an
  ordinary pending offer with its new saving.
- After 10 minutes, if the same route is still faster, it is a new offer:
  the pending banner reads "Still faster. Checking again." / "Would save
  8 min · 2 fewer lights" and the flow continues as usual, Keep my route
  included.
- Stages: `merged-declined`, `merged-again`, `merged-loop-keep` (the switch
  loop ending with the button pressed).

### Partial and unknown light counts while driving (#44)

Lights appear in two places while driving, and both follow the card rules:

- **More sheet, "Lights to go"**: full "21"; partial "≥ 9" with "data
  missing" beside it; unknown "Unknown". The tile keeps one height in every
  state so the sheet never moves, and it never shows 0.
- **Pending banner**: the second line carries the tiebreak, "Would save 8 min
  · 2 fewer lights". On a partial or unknown route it reads "Would save 8 min
  · lights: can't compare". The title is now "Checking a faster route", which
  says the stage in words.
- **Confirmed banner**: unchanged in every state. It is the engine's spoken
  sentence and that sentence does not mention lights.
- The collapsed driving sheet shows no light count at all (two numbers only).
- Stages: `merged-signals-driving`, `merged-partial-pending`,
  `merged-partial-more`, `merged-unknown-more`.

### Turn card when there is no turn soon (#45)

- The hero is always the **distance to the next manoeuvre**, even when it is
  far: "12 mi" with a straight arrow. The old "Straight on / No turn for a
  while" is gone. (12 mi is the sample figure from the issue.)
- The line under it is a plain instruction in the sentence face: "Continue
  straight", or "Continue on Johnston Rd" when the road name is known. A
  street to turn onto keeps the condensed face.
- The card has the same layout in both cases, so nothing moves when a switch
  arrives: the arrow turns and 12 mi becomes 0.8 mi.
- Two numbers at a glance still holds: 12 mi and 43 min.
- Ten miles and over reads as whole miles; under ten, one decimal.
- The voice says the same thing: "Continue for 12 miles." / "Continue on
  Johnston Road for 12 miles." / "In 0.8 miles, turn right onto Johnston
  Road."
- Stages: `merged-straight`, `merged-straight-day`, `merged-turn-cards`.

### No blank frame at the loop reset (#46)

- Every loop (the three explorations, the comparison, the merged switch and
  the Keep-my-route ending) now resets with a cross-fade. A still copy of the
  opening frame sits under the live screen; at the reset the live content
  fades out over it, the trip is put back unseen, and the live screen returns
  at once as the same picture. Nothing is ever blank and nothing jumps.
- Checks after the change: the `near-empty frames` line is gone from every
  loop and the gate passes. The jump scan reports no screen element jumping.
  What it still prints is the kit's own odometer (`Count`) moving its digit
  strip back to the middle after a roll, inside its clipped window
  (`jump: span "0".."9"`, `settle snap: span`), and `appears with no
  entrance: div.rq-body`, which is the live screen returning over an
  identical frame. Coverage is still `inconclusive` because headless Chrome
  drops frames on this machine; the seam was also stepped at 80 ms with the
  clock slowed 5x and read frame by frame.
- All GIFs and contact sheets were re-exported.

## Ground rules the screens keep

- The app knows where traffic lights are, not their colour: lights are neutral rings or beads, never red, amber or green.
- Only the real sample numbers appear: 35 min / 21 mi / 21 lights, 43 min / 24 mi / 23 lights, 0.8 mi to Johnston Rd, 8 min saved. Arrival times are 9:41 plus the trip time.
- Text contrast is 4.5:1 or better in every direction (lowest pair: plum on coral, 5.2:1). Driving controls are 60 px or larger. Slow stretches are hatched and labelled, the chosen route carries a tick and words, the switch is announced in words: colour is never the only signal.
